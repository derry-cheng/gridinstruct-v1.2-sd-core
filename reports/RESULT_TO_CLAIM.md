## What the local evidence supports

The current package supports the scale, task inventory, identifier uniqueness, row-level contracts, rule links, split-key checks, registered OPF subset checks, and reproducible CPU/MPS baseline metrics stated in the machine-readable reports. The new task-balanced instruction-surface split contains 14,257/4,735/4,696 train/validation/test records and 71,791 OOD records, with all six task families represented in its test partition. Exact normalized user-instruction collisions are zero across its train/validation/test partitions.

These results establish a traceable benchmark candidate and task learnability diagnostics. They do not establish full-population physical truth, semantic independence, expert target validity, executable dispatch, or public reproducibility.

The task-balanced regime also includes a probability-producing logistic TF--IDF reference with hash-bound test/OOD calibration outputs and a 44-variant input-field/keyword-mask audit. These additions quantify confidence quality and shortcut sensitivity for the local reference models; they do not convert local labels into physical truth.

## Required interpretation

The MinHash split is a detected-component proxy. The exact character-Jaccard audit shows residual high lexical similarity in deterministic samples, so neither MinHash nor instruction-surface grouping is described as a semantic de-duplication oracle. Structured-query and auxiliary-tool 1.000 values are contract-predictability or deterministic serialization diagnostics. The 320-record/160-scenario OPF evidence is registered released-field and fixed-control replay evidence; it does not replace the missing full scenario and candidate ledgers.
