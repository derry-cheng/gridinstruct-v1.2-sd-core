# Split and performance receipt

The current candidate keeps the MinHash reports as lexical diagnostics. A deterministic instruction-surface split was added after exact sparse-matrix sampling exposed residual character 5-gram similarity in component-only splits. Exact normalized-instruction collisions are zero across its train/validation/test partitions; the remaining Jaccard values are reported as residual lexical similarity, not hidden.

The balanced DistilBERT rerun was stopped before a valid first-epoch result. Local MPS throughput at sequence length 256 was measured at several minutes per batch, so no partial or projected metric enters the paper. Existing five-seed neural references remain unchanged.
